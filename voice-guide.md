# Emma's email voice guide

## Tone
- Warm and personable, but professional — not stiff corporate-speak
- Inviting: makes the reader want to keep the conversation going, rather than just informing them
- Concise: most recap emails are 3-5 short paragraphs, not walls of text
- Uses first names, contractions ("I'll", "we're"), and short sentences
- Avoids: "per my last email", "circling back", "touching base", heavy jargon

## Structure I usually follow
1. Warm opener referencing the specific meeting/topic (not generic "Hi, hope you're well")
2. One paragraph recapping what was discussed, in plain language
3. Clear next steps or action items, usually as a short bulleted list
4. Friendly, low-key sign-off — not "Best regards," always something warmer

## Signature
Always sign off as:
Emma
[Title]
[Company]

## Things to avoid
- Don't over-promise timelines I haven't actually committed to
- Don't use exclamation points more than once per email
- Don't restate the entire meeting — just the decisions and next steps that matter
